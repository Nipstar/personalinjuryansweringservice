What is a personal injury answering service?
A personal injury answering service takes a law firm's calls when the office is closed. Most take a name, a number and a message. An intake specialist goes further, asking what happened and when, what the injuries are, what treatment has followed and who was at fault, so an attorney can judge the case before the first callback.
Where do injury calls go after the office closes?
Accidents don't keep office hours, and neither do the calls that follow them. For most PI practices, an after-hours call ends in one of three places.
	•	Voicemail, where many callers hang up and try the next firm on the results page.
	•	A message service, which passes on a name, a number and "please call back", but nothing about the case.
	•	Your own cell phone, answered at 10pm, at dinner and at weekends, for as long as you can keep it up.
What does a missed injury call cost?
In Clio's 2024 secret-shopper study of 500 US law firms, only 40% answered when a prospective client called [source: Clio Legal Trends Report 2024]. An injured caller rarely waits for the office to open. They call the next firm, and the marketing spend that made your phone ring goes with them.

What does a virtual receptionist for lawyers do?
A virtual receptionist for lawyers answers a firm's phone remotely when the office is busy or closed. The basic service takes a name, a number and a message. An intake specialist goes further, asking the questions that tell an attorney whether there is a case, and flagging the calls that can't wait until morning.
Receptionist or intake specialist?
Most lawyers who search for a virtual receptionist are really looking for intake. A receptionist answers the phone. An intake specialist answers the question you ask the next morning: is there a case here, and how soon do I need to call back?
Della is an AI intake specialist, built for personal injury, criminal defense and immigration calls. A live receptionist at a call center is the better fit when most of your calls need judgment no intake protocol can supply, such as complex commercial matters.
